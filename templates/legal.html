Last updated {{ updated }}

This explains what {{ site }} collects, why, and what we don't do with it.

1. What we collect

If you create an account: your email address, and the display name and profile picture your sign-in provider shares. Nothing else.

Sign-in codes: when you sign in by email, we store a hashed version of the one-time code and its expiry. It is deleted the moment it's used or expires.

Server logs: our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs, which may include IP addresses and error details. These are used to keep the service running and diagnose faults.

2. What we don't do

  • We don't build advertising profiles.
  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't store a permanent history of your searches linked to your account.
  • We don't ask for passwords — sign-in happens through your provider or a one-time code.

3. Searches

Your query is sent to a third-party search provider so results can be returned. That provider handles the query under its own privacy policy. When you open a page in the reader view, our server fetches it on your behalf, which means the destination site sees our server rather than your browser.

4. Cookies

{{ site }} uses a single cookie to keep you signed in. It contains a signed session identifier, not personal data. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. Signing out clears it.

5. Third parties we rely on

  • Sign-in providers (Google, GitHub, GitLab, Microsoft, Discord) — they tell us your email, name, and picture when you choose to sign in. They don't receive your searches.
  • Email delivery — used only to send sign-in codes.
  • Search providers — receive your query.
  • Hosting — runs the service and keeps technical logs.

6. How long we keep things

Account records are kept until you ask us to delete them. Sign-in codes last no more than 10 minutes. Server logs follow our host's retention schedule.

7. Your choices

You can sign out at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account and its email address. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, or export your data, or to object to processing.

8. Children

{{ site }} isn't intended for children under 13. If we learn we've collected an account from a child under 13, we delete it.

9. Security

Sign-in codes are hashed, sessions are signed, and traffic is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute protection.
